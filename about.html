Our Mission

Why We Sing

Opera À la Carte, San Diego's mission is to provide opportunities for performers to showcase their talent as a commitment to promoting the performing arts and classical music in the greater San Diego area. O.A.C. strives to captivate our audience with engaging and accessible performances with the hope of creating new opera lovers. We want everyone to have the opportunity to experience the beauty and magic of opera and classical singing. We will enrich and educate our community through storytelling, outreach and by aspiring to artistic excellence.

What Guides Us

Our Values

Excellence

Artistic excellence and high quality performances in everything we stage.

Community

Engagement with our local community — opera belongs to everyone.

Collaboration

Partnering and collaborating with venues and artist organizations across San Diego.

Relevance

Keeping opera and classical singing current for today's audiences.

Access

Growing a broader, more diverse audience — new opera lovers welcome.

Our Vision

Looking Ahead

Our vision is to produce shows of artistic excellence that have positive impact on our community. We endeavor to educate, to entertain and to demystify opera and classical singing. We strive to sing for diverse audiences, as well as forge artistic collaborations that promote the arts throughout greater San Diego.
