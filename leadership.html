Leadership

The women guiding Opera à la Carte San Diego.

Abla Hamza

Co-Founder · Executive & Artistic Director · Board President

Abla Lynn Hamza is a professional soprano whose career includes principal roles with Sarasota Opera, Opera Southwest, and St. Petersburg Opera, as well as secondary, comprimario and bit roles with major companies such as Dallas Opera, Fort Worth Opera, and San Diego Opera.

She is the Co-Founder, Executive/Artistic Director, and current Board President of Opera À la Carte San Diego, a women-led opera company dedicated to making opera more accessible, inclusive, and community-driven. In its first year and a half as a nonprofit, the company has presented over fifteen concerts, created performance opportunities for nearly 50 local singers, and produced its first fully staged opera to critical acclaim.

In addition to her performance career, Abla brings a growing foundation in arts leadership. She previously founded the Philadelphia chapter of Opera on Tap, producing concerts throughout the city, including at the Barnes Foundation and the Old City Festival. She also worked as a public relations assistant at the Curtis Institute of Music for three years, gaining valuable experience in institutional communications and arts management.

Abla is deeply committed to building a vibrant opera culture in San Diego — one rooted in collaboration, excellence, and artistic equity.

Chelsea Seener Gornall

Co-Founder · Director of Programs/Concerts · Board Treasurer

As both an operatic performer and experienced bookkeeper, Chelsea Seener Gornall is all about finding harmony between creativity and organization. Focused on bridging financial strategy with artistic vision, she offers a unique dual perspective as Treasurer with Opera à la Carte.

A versatile vocalist, Chelsea's voice spans the lyric drama of opera, the heart of musical theatre, and the boldness of rock. She has been a featured soloist across the U.S. and has performed internationally in six countries. Born and raised in San Diego, she's proud to be building a vibrant opera community in the city where she first took the stage.

While music has always been her primary focus, Chelsea has over a decade of experience as a bookkeeper and office manager. Her leadership began early — serving as a cultural ambassador in high school and in an exchange program with Japan, helping launch her college's Student NATS chapter, and being one of the first educational outreach performers for San Diego Opera; she's been lifting up the arts since day one. She now supports Opera à la Carte both onstage and off, and is proud to be part of a nonprofit that brings people together through music and shared purpose with even more exciting things on the horizon!

Barbara Mortkowitz

Board Member · Board Secretary

Barbara is a graduate of Dominican University in San Rafael, California, where she earned a Masters Degree in Humanities, focusing on Russian composer Dimitri Shostakovich as well as the cantorial tradition up to Hildegard von Bingen.

At the California College of the Arts, she studied Environmental Design and subsequently had a career in the field of commercial interior design, and volunteered in community theatres as a prop and set designer.

As a child, she studied classical piano, was constantly surrounded by music — attending operas in San Francisco and the San Jose Symphony. She majored in music in her first year of college and later worked in the music department at Stanford University in community relations and outreach.

Most recently in Northern California, she served on various boards and commissions related to the arts and developed a practice for exhibiting the works of both book artists and visual artists. As an opera lover, Barbara is very excited and honored to join Opera à la Carte as a board member — getting involved in its growth and success as well as being a part of the wonderful opera community.
