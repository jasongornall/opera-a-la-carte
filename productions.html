Latest Production · September 2026

Viva la Mamma!

Step behind the curtain and into the wonderfully chaotic world of opera with Donizetti's Viva la Mamma! — a fast-paced comedy that affectionately spoofs the larger-than-life personalities, backstage drama, and theatrical mishaps that can unfold when an opera company prepares for opening night!

Whether you're a lifelong opera lover or experiencing opera for the very first time, this fast-paced comedy offers an evening of laughter, memorable music, and a playful glimpse into the delightful madness that can happen behind the curtain.

Directed by Adam Cioffari with Music Direction by Carl Pantle.

Performances: September 18 & 19 at 7pm, September 20 at 3pm
Venue: The Light Box Theater at Liberty Station, San Diego

Tickets via TicketLeap
