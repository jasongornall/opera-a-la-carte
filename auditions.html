Most Recent Audition Call

Viva la Mamma!

Auditions: Saturday, May 2 · 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
San Diego State University – School of Music, Room M208

Opera À la Carte San Diego accepted audition submissions for Donizetti's Viva la Mamma (or Le convenienze ed inconvenienze teatrali). We sought singers of all voice types for principal and supporting roles. This is a comedic opera, and we were especially interested in performers with strong acting skills, comic timing, and a willingness to engage in physical comedy and ensemble work.

To apply: complete the audition form and upload your headshot and resume:

Open Audition Form

Please prepare: 2–3 arias, including one in Italian (preferably Rossini, Donizetti, or Bellini) and one that highlights your acting ability. More details are included in the application.

Questions? Email us anytime.
